Frequently asked questions

How do I choose the correct size or pack?

Open the product page and select the pack size before adding it to your cart. Price and availability update for the selected option.

How can I track my order?

Sign in and open My Account → Orders. Each order shows its current status and complete purchase details.

Can I buy veterinary medicines normally?

Products marked “Rx” require a valid veterinary prescription and cannot use ordinary checkout. Contact support for assistance.

What if my order arrives damaged or incorrect?

Contact us within 48 hours of delivery with your order number and clear photographs of the package and item.

Can I save products for later?

Yes. Sign in, open a product and choose Save Product. It will appear under Saved in your account.

{% elif page_key == 'shipping' %}DELIVERY WITH CARE

Shipping & delivery

We pack pet-care products carefully and provide order status updates through your account.

Order processing

Orders are processed after payment confirmation, or after placement for Cash on Delivery. Availability and serviceability can affect dispatch times.

Delivery estimates

The delivery estimate shown during checkout is indicative. Weekends, public holidays, severe weather, courier disruptions and remote locations can add time.

Address accuracy

Please confirm the recipient name, mobile number, full address and PIN code before ordering. Contact support promptly if a correction is needed; changes may not be possible after dispatch.

Damaged parcels

If a parcel appears damaged, photograph it before opening. Report damaged, leaking, missing or incorrect items within 48 hours of delivery.

{% elif page_key == 'returns' %}FAIR & CLEAR

Returns & refunds

Important: For hygiene and safety, opened food, grooming products, supplements and medicines are generally not returnable.

Eligible requests

Contact us within 48 hours if an item is damaged, leaking, expired, incorrect or missing. Keep the original packaging and share clear photographs.

Change-of-mind returns

Unopened, unused, non-medicinal products may be considered if requested within 7 days of delivery. Approval depends on product condition and category. Return shipping may apply.

Non-returnable items

Prescription medicines, temperature-sensitive products, opened consumables, used hygiene products, and items without original packaging or labels cannot be accepted unless damaged or incorrect on arrival.

Refund timing

Approved refunds are sent to the original payment method. Bank or payment-provider processing time may vary. Cash-on-delivery refunds may require verified bank details.

{% elif page_key == 'veterinary' %}🩺 PET SAFETY FIRST

Veterinary medicine policy

This store does not replace a veterinarian.

Product descriptions are general information and are not diagnosis, dosage instructions or medical advice.

Prescription products

Items marked “Rx” require a valid prescription from a registered veterinarian. We may verify the prescription and refuse or cancel supply where requirements are not met.

Use medicines safely

Use products only for the species, weight and condition directed by your veterinarian and the manufacturer’s label. Never change dosage or combine treatments without professional guidance.

Emergencies and adverse reactions

For poisoning, breathing difficulty, collapse, seizures, severe allergic reaction or any urgent symptoms, contact a veterinarian or emergency veterinary service immediately.

Storage

Follow package storage instructions and keep all medicines away from children and animals. Do not use products after their expiry date or if packaging integrity is compromised.
